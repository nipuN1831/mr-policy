What happens if we change a rule?

      
The same synthetic applicants are decided under the old rule and the new one. Nobody's behaviour is predicted, people are only re-decided.

      

        
        
        
      

      

      

    

  


  

    

      
Does the software follow the policy?

      
Tests are built from the rule's own boundaries: for each condition, two nearly identical people on either side of the line. One implementation is correct, and ten each hide one realistic bug.
