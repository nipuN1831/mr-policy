What the headline doesn't tell you

Official names are often simpler than the rules behind them. Here is what the text actually says, verified from government pages.

Ujjwala PMUY 2.0 ✓ Checkable

What it says

"Free LPG connection for poor women."

What the rule actually means

Free connection only — not the cylinder or gas. You must have no existing LPG connection from any oil company in your household. You also need to submit a deprivation declaration form, not just an Aadhaar card.

Who the rules exclude

Men. Households with any OMC connection already registered. Anyone who cannot produce the declaration form in the prescribed format.

Verified from pmuy.gov.in — Oct 2026

Citizenship Amendment Act 2019 ✓ Checkable

What it says

"Citizenship path for persecuted minorities from neighbouring countries."

What the rule actually means

Applies to 6 religions only (Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi, Christian) — not Muslim, Jewish, or atheist. You must have entered India on or before 31 December 2014. You still need 5 years of continuous residency before applying.

Who the rules exclude

Anyone from a country other than Afghanistan, Bangladesh or Pakistan. Residents of Inner Line Permit states (Arunachal, Nagaland, Mizoram, Manipur) and Sixth Schedule tribal areas.

Source: PRS Legislative Research, Act No. 47 of 2019

Right to Information Act 2005 ℹ Civic info

What it says

"Any citizen can ask for government information."

What the rule actually means

The central portal only accepts central government requests — filing a state matter here gets it returned with no refund. RTI does not cover RAW, IB, and certain security agencies. The PIO has 30 days to reply, not indefinitely.

What most people miss

Cabinet papers, trade secrets, and information that would prejudice law enforcement can be withheld. A first appeal and then a second appeal to the Central Information Commission are available if your request is refused.

Verified from rtionline.gov.in — Oct 2026

RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme ℹ Civic info

What it says

"Free and quick resolution for banking complaints."

What the rule actually means

You must complain to the bank first and wait for their response (or 30 days) before the Ombudsman will accept your case. Keep your reference number — without it, the complaint is not admissible.

What most people miss

Does not cover disputes about the bank's internal policy, loans outside its control, or matters already in a court of law. The scheme covers banks, NBFCs and payment systems — not insurance or mutual funds.

Source: cms.rbi.org.in — Oct 2026
