How it works

  1. Write the rule down
    A policy text becomes a contract of exact conditions, each tied to the sentence it came from.
  2. A person approves
    Unclear wording is flagged. A person decides what it means before anything is treated as a bug.
  3. Run it every way
    The same contract decides citizens, simulates changes and generates boundary tests.
  4. Show the evidence
    Every answer quotes the clause, with its source and date.
